Account Deletion Request

MM-Elibrary

Last updated: September 14, 2026

You can request deletion of your MM-Elibrary account and associated personal data using the steps below.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to leolin231665@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: MM-Elibrary Account Deletion Request
  3. Include the Google account email (or username) you used to sign in to MM-Elibrary
  4. Confirm that you want your account and associated data deleted
Contact email: leolin231665@gmail.com

What data is deleted

When your request is processed, we delete or anonymize:

  • Account profile information (name, email, username)
  • Authentication / login data linked to your account
  • App activity data tied to your account (for example favorites and reading history), where stored on our servers

What may be retained

We may retain limited records only when required by law, security, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution. Retained data is kept only as long as necessary for those purposes.

Processing time

We aim to process account deletion requests within 30 days of verifying your request.

Partial data deletion

If you want specific data removed but wish to keep your account, email leolin231665@gmail.com with details of your request. We will review and respond.
